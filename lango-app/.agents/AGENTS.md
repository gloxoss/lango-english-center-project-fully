# SchoolOS (SchoolOS) Workspace Rules & Color Guidelines

## NON-NEGOTIABLE COLOR & BRAND RULES

1. **NO GREEN IN THE APP**:
   - Green (`#17A673`, `#DDF5EC`, `#10B981`, `emerald-500`, etc.) is **COMPLETELY PROHIBITED** across all pages, UI components, badges, charts, and buttons.
   - **Deep Cerulean Blue (`#2487B8`)** is the primary brand color and MUST be used for all success metrics, positive trends, fee collection indicators, badge states, and primary actions.

2. **RESERVED COLOR ALLOCATIONS**:
   - **Primary Action / Positive Success**: **Deep Cerulean Blue (`#2487B8`)** with soft tint background (`#E4EDFD`).
   - **Headlines & Text**: **Ink (`#16212B`)**.
   - **Product Canvas**: **Cerulean Mist (`#EDF3F8`)** and clean white (`#FFFFFF`) card containers.
   - **Automated System SMS**: **Signal Cyan (`#0EA5C4`)** (Reserved *only* for automated messaging).
   - **Warning / Exception States (Rare)**: Amber `#E8A33D` (soft tint `#FCF0DC`) and Red `#E5544B` (soft tint `#FCE4E2`).

3. **MINIMALIST DESIGN DISCIPLINE**:
   - Keep interfaces clean, editorial, and uncluttered.
   - Avoid heavy background fills or rainbow badges.
   - Use high-contrast typography, tabular numbers for money (`187 450 MAD`), and subtle slate borders (`border-slate-200`).
