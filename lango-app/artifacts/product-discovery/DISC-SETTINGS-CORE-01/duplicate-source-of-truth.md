Measured 2026-09-25, commit 54d386a4, dev DB `schoolos`. Each row: what is duplicated, where each copy lives, who reads which copy, and the proof.

| # | Concept | Copy A | Copy B (and C) | Who reads what | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
