# S-38 · HR self-service locked for every employee

**Severity: P1** · Source report: [2026-09-23-seeded-db-role-and-addon-audit.md](../../2026-09-23-seeded-db-role-and-addon-audit.md) · Pages affected: 1

**Code check (2026-09-23):** STILL OPEN: `payroll.self.read` is still in no role default; the view still maps any 403 to "not an employee".

## What is wrong

Teacher `prof.01` (active employee profile) sees "Aucun profil employé associé à ce compte". 403 on `/api/employee/me/{payroll,advances,awards}`.

## Why

`payroll.self.read` is in no role's `DEFAULT_ROLE_PERMISSIONS` (`libs/api/permissions.ts:296`); `employee-portal-view.tsx:182` maps any 403 to "not an employee".

## Fix

Grant `payroll.self.read` to employee roles; only show "not an employee" for `error.code === NOT_AN_EMPLOYEE`; hide just the failing section otherwise.

## Plan

1. Add the permission to teacher, accountant, receptionist, guard, librarian, school_admin.
2. Change the error mapping.
3. Test: active teacher loads payslips.

## Done when

`prof.01` sees payslips, advances and awards.
