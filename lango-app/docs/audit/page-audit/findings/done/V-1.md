## What is wrong

Finance home: 6 000 MAD / 2 overdue invoices. Dashboard, invoices and finance reminders all agree on 3 000 MAD / 1.

## Why

`/api/accountant/me/home` computes overdue with its own formula instead of `libs/finance/definitions.ts` (counts not-yet-due balances).

## Fix

Make `/api/accountant/me/home`, `/api/accountant/me/receivables`, `features/portal/services/portal-home.ts` and `api/students/route.ts` use the shared definitions: overdue = past due only; balance = netAmount - paidAmount; collected = posted, net of refunds; branch filter applied.

## Plan

1. Replace the local overdue query in `/api/accountant/me/home` with the shared helper.
2. Do the same in the three other call sites.
3. Add a test: one past-due and one not-yet-due invoice give overdue = the past-due one only, on every endpoint.
4. Re-sweep `/dashboard/finance` and `/dashboard` and compare the numbers.

## Done when

Finance home, dashboard, invoices and reminders show the same overdue amount and count.
